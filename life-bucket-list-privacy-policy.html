1Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Life Bucket List (package name com.goalvault.bucketlist), an Android app that helps you create, organize, track and complete your life goals. The app is provided by VERTEX LIT (PRIVATE) LIMITED ("we", "us" or "our").

We built Life Bucket List to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

2Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal or non-personal information from you. We have no servers that receive data from the app, and we never have access to the goals you create.

Information you enter in the app

When you use Life Bucket List, you may enter information about your goals. This information is stored only on your device, in the app's private database, and is never sent to us:

Goal information stored on your device
InformationPurpose
Goal title and optional descriptionShows the goal on your list
Category (for example Travel or Career) and priorityOrganizes your goals and powers the Insights screen
Optional target dateShows the date and the days left until it
Completion status, creation date and completion dateMoves completed goals to History and calculates your progress

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information (such as health, financial or identity details) in your goals. Although it stays on your device, anyone with access to your unlocked phone could see it.

Information we do not collect

  • Name, email address or phone number Not collected
  • Location Not collected
  • Contacts, photos, files or media Not collected
  • Device identifiers or advertising ID Not collected
  • Usage analytics or app activity Not collected
  • Crash logs or diagnostics Not collected

3How Information Is Used

The goal information you enter is used only by the app, on your device, to:

  • Display, edit and delete your goals.
  • Mark goals as complete and show them in your History.
  • Calculate the statistics shown on the Insights screen, such as your progress by category and priority.
  • Show target dates and how many days are left.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, because it never leaves your device for us.

4App Permissions

Life Bucket List does not request any sensitive Android permissions. In particular, the app does not request access to:

  • Internet Not requested
  • Location Not requested
  • Camera or microphone Not requested
  • Contacts Not requested
  • Photos, media or files Not requested
  • Notifications Not requested

Because the app has no internet permission, it cannot connect to the internet or transmit your data anywhere on its own.

5Device Backup

Life Bucket List supports Android's built-in backup feature. If you have turned on backup for your device (for example, backup to your Google Account), Android may include the app's data, including your goals, in that backup so it can be restored when you set up a new phone or reinstall the app.

This backup is performed by Android and your backup provider, not by us. We cannot access these backups. Backups are handled under the privacy policy of your backup provider, such as the Google Privacy Policy.

6Third-Party Services

Life Bucket List does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, authentication, payment, cloud or AI services. It does not use Firebase, Google Sign-In or any similar service.

The app is built with standard Android libraries from Google (Android Jetpack and Material Components). These libraries run on your device and do not collect or send your information.

Links and actions that open other apps

The Settings screen contains three options that hand you over to another app. The app itself sends no information when you use them:

  • Rate Us opens the app's page in the Google Play Store.
  • Privacy Policy opens this page in your web browser.
  • Share App opens Android's share menu with a short message and a link to the app on Google Play. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app and send the message yourself. Your goals are never included.

Once you are in another app, such as Google Play, your browser or a messaging app, that app's own privacy policy applies. We encourage you to review those policies.

7Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your information with anyone, because we do not collect it. Your goals are not transferred to us or to any third party by the app.

8Data Retention and Deletion

Your goals stay on your device until you delete them. You can remove your data at any time:

  • Delete a single goal with the delete button on its card, on the Home or History screen. Deleted goals cannot be recovered from within the app.
  • Delete all app data by going to your phone's Settings > Apps > Life Bucket List > Storage > Clear storage.
  • Uninstall the app, which removes all of its data from your device.

If your device backup is turned on, a copy of the app's data may remain in your backup until your backup provider updates or deletes it. You can manage or delete backups through your device or backup provider settings.

9Data Security

Your goals are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Android's app sandbox prevents other apps from reading this storage. Because the app does not send your data over the internet, it is not exposed to risks from data transmission or server storage.

The security of the data on your phone also depends on your device. We recommend protecting your phone with a screen lock and keeping its software up to date. No method of storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10Children's Privacy

Life Bucket List is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because the app does not collect personal information at all. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about your child's use of the app, please contact us using the details below.

11Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, such as the right to access, correct, delete or move it. Because your goals are stored only on your device and we hold no copy, you can exercise these rights directly in the app:

  • Access: view all your goals on the Home, History and Insights screens.
  • Correct: edit any goal at any time.
  • Delete: delete individual goals, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Control backups: turn device backup on or off in your phone's settings.

If you have any questions about your privacy rights, please contact us.

12Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Life Bucket List app does not use cookies, web beacons or similar tracking technologies. This Privacy Policy page does not set cookies or use tracking scripts either.

13Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features to the app. When we do, we will post the updated policy on this page and change the "Last updated" date at the top. If a change significantly affects how the app handles your information, we will make reasonable efforts to let you know, such as through the app or its Google Play listing.

We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of the app after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
